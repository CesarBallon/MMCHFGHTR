# Security policy

## Reporting

Do not open public issues for vulnerabilities that could expose accounts, credentials, private player data, ranking integrity or live services. Until a private reporting address is configured, report them directly to the project owner through the established private project channel.

The future shared repository must enable GitHub private vulnerability reporting before public launch and replace this paragraph with its exact reporting route and response targets.

## Scope priorities

- account takeover or authorization bypass
- forged, duplicated or manipulated ranked results
- leaked deployment, database or third-party credentials
- cross-site scripting or unsafe user-generated content
- denial of service against matchmaking or matches
- exposure of private profiles, reports, replays or moderation records

Never test against other players or production data without explicit written authorization. Use isolated test accounts and environments.
